Some projects feel like instant sunshine. This cheerful sunflower sits above a crocheted pen sleeve and a tiny pot, turning ordinary stationery into a miniature desk garden. The puffed seed ring and layered yellow petals make the bloom especially tactile.

<h2 id="before-you-begin">Your desk-garden project at a glance</h2>

- **Difficulty:** Advanced (source pattern rating).
- **Approximate finished size:** Approximately 12 × 25 cm; measurements depend on pen, yarn, tension, and flower shaping.
- **Hook:** 3.75 mm or 4 mm. **Terms:** US crochet terms; work in continuous rounds unless specified otherwise.
- **Construction:** individual flower head, two leaves, green pen sleeve, terracotta pot, and hollow-centered soil.
- **Before stitching:** choose a pen and confirm its barrel width. The base pattern is sized for a pen around 8 mm across.

1. Sew the finished blossom securely to the top of the green pen sleeve. Position it so the bloom faces forward.
2. Add the two leaves to opposite sides of the sleeve, where your flower instructions indicate.
3. Set the crocheted soil piece into the pot and secure it with small, tidy stitches around the edge so it stays in place.
4. Guide the green pen cover through the central opening of the soil. Check that the pen tip remains usable and that the flower head is balanced.
5. Weave in loose yarn ends and gently shape the petals with your fingers—no heavy pulling.

> **Fit and safety:** This is a decorative pen accessory, not a toy for small children. Test the diameter and length against the actual pen, avoid blocking the writing tip, and secure small parts carefully.

<h2 id="maker-faq">A few questions before you finish</h2>

**Can I use another pen?** Yes. The source gives the tube for a roughly 8 mm pen. A wider pen needs more starting stitches and the corresponding number of sc in subsequent rounds; make a quick fit check first.

**Why does the soil have a hole?** The inward-folded tube creates an opening for the pen-cover stem; stuffing the center solid would prevent that.

**Can I change the pot color?** Absolutely. Coral, terracotta, blush pink, and moss green all suit this little desk-garden collection.
