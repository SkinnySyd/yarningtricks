This simple granny square makes a lovely practice project. Make a few and try changing colors as you go! These instructions use **US crochet terms**.

## Before you start

You'll use a **chain (ch)**, **slip stitch (sl st)**, and **double crochet (dc)**. A *granny cluster* is three double-crochet stitches worked into the same space.

> Tip: Work the square loosely for your first try. You can tighten things up on the next one.

## Step-by-step pattern

### Round 1 — Make four corners

1. Make a magic ring. If that's tricky, chain 4 and slip stitch to form a ring instead.
2. Chain 3 (counts as your first dc), then work 2 dc into the ring. Chain 2.
3. Repeat **(3 dc, ch 2)** into the ring **three more times**.
4. Pull the ring closed if you used a magic ring. Slip stitch to the top of the beginning chain 3 to join.

You should have four groups of three double-crochet stitches, with four chain-2 corner spaces.

### Round 2 — Build the square

1. Slip stitch into the next corner space.
2. Chain 3 (counts as first dc); work 2 dc, chain 2, and 3 dc into that same corner. Chain 1.
3. In each of the next three corner spaces, work **(3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc)**, followed by chain 1.
4. Slip stitch into the top of the starting chain 3 to join.

Now your square should have eight granny clusters and four defined corners. Weave in the yarn ends.

## Finishing touches

Gently shape your square and lightly block it if your yarn allows. For a larger coaster or mat, continue adding rounds by placing **(3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc)** in every corner space and **3 dc** into each chain-1 side space, with chain 1 between groups.

## Make it yours

Try one shade for the center and a contrasting shade on the second round. Handmade coasters make lovely small gifts.
