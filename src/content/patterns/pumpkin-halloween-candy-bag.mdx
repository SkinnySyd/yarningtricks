Welcome, autumn! This little candy carrier begins as a flat round base, grows into a plump, ribbed pumpkin shape, and finishes with a softly textured rim and a separate handle. The original design uses **orange yarn throughout**—no stitched-on face, leaves or closure required. Let the distinctive raised columns do the decorating.

<h2 id="before-you-begin">Before you pick up your hook</h2>

<div className="pumpkin-facts">
  <div><span>01 / Difficulty</span><strong>Intermediate</strong><small>Front-post and reverse stitches</small></div>
  <div><span>02 / Finished size</span><strong>About 7 × 11 in</strong><small>Height × width, as stated in the source</small></div>
  <div><span>03 / Hook</span><strong>5 mm · H/8</strong><small>Use US crochet terms</small></div>
  <div><span>04 / Construction</span><strong>In joined rounds</strong><small>Handle worked separately in rows</small></div>
</div>

The pattern begins with a **magic ring**. The starting chain-2 counts as a stitch throughout the joined-round body unless a round says otherwise. At the end of each round, join as directed. Place a stitch marker at the round join to help you track your increases and decreases.

<h3 id="stitch-key">Your crochet shorthand (US terms)</h3>

| Abbreviation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ch | Chain |
| st / sts | Stitch / stitches |
| hdc | Half double crochet |
| sc | Single crochet |
| FPdc | Front-post double crochet, worked around the post of the stitch below |
| hdc2tog | Half-double-crochet two stitches together (decrease) |
| BLO | Back loop only |
| rev sc | Reverse single crochet (crab stitch) |

<div className="pumpkin-tip"><strong>🍁 Two special stitches to know</strong><p>For <b>FPdc</b>, yarn over, insert your hook from front to back to front around the specified stitch post, draw up a loop, and finish a double crochet. For <b>hdc2tog</b>, yarn over, draw up a loop in each of the next two stitches, then yarn over and draw through all five loops. These raised and decreasing stitches create the pumpkin's texture and curve.</p></div>

<h2 id="make-the-pieces">Part 1 · Start with the round base</h2>

<figure className="instruction-photo pumpkin-feature-photo"><img src="/images/tutorials/pumpkin-halloween-bag/base-rounds.webp" alt="Illustrated concentric crochet rounds increasing from six to thirty-six stitches" width="960" height="680" loading="lazy"/><figcaption><span>ILLUSTRATION</span> The first six rounds spread out into a circular base. The illustration shows the growth, not a photographed stitch-by-stitch sequence.</figcaption></figure>

Keep working in orange yarn. The early rounds expand by **six stitches each time**.

| Round | What to crochet | Total |
|---|---|---:|
| Foundation | Make a magic ring. Ch 2 (this counts as a stitch), work 5 hdc into the ring, then join. | 6 |
| 1 | Ch 2, 1 hdc in the joining stitch, then 2 hdc in each remaining stitch; join. | 12 |
| 2 | Ch 2, 1 hdc in joining stitch, 1 hdc in next stitch; repeat **(2 hdc in next stitch, 1 hdc in next stitch)** around; join. | 18 |
| 3 | Ch 2, 1 hdc in joining stitch, 1 hdc in each of the next 2 stitches; repeat **(2 hdc in next stitch, 1 hdc in each of next 2 stitches)** around; join. | 24 |
| 4 | Ch 2, 1 hdc in joining stitch, 1 hdc in each of the next 3 stitches; repeat **(2 hdc in next stitch, 1 hdc in each of next 3 stitches)** around; join. | 30 |
| 5 | Ch 2, 1 hdc in joining stitch, 1 hdc in each of the next 4 stitches; repeat **(2 hdc in next stitch, 1 hdc in each of next 4 stitches)** around; join. | 36 |

<h2 id="pumpkin-ribs">Part 2 · Give the pumpkin its raised ribs</h2>

<figure className="instruction-photo pumpkin-feature-photo"><img src="/images/tutorials/pumpkin-halloween-bag/ribbed-body.webp" alt="Illustration of the ribbed texture created by front-post stitches along the crochet bag body" width="960" height="680" loading="lazy"/><figcaption><span>TEXTURE GUIDE</span> The raised columns are made with front-post double crochets. Use the written rounds for precise placement.</figcaption></figure>

The next rounds turn your base upward, then create the vertical lines that make the fabric resemble a real pumpkin. The original design alternates textured rounds with rows that increase the circumference.

| Round | What to crochet | Total |
|---|---|---:|
| 6 | Ch 2; working **in back loops only**, hdc once in every stitch; join. | 36 |
| 7 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 2 stitches, 2 hdc in next stitch, hdc in next 3 stitches**; join. | 42 |
| 8 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 7 stitches**; join. | 42 |
| 9 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 3 stitches, 2 hdc in next stitch, hdc in next 3 stitches**; join. | 48 |
| 10 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 8 stitches**; join. | 48 |
| 11 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 3 stitches, 2 hdc in next stitch, hdc in next 4 stitches**; join. | 54 |
| 12–14 | On each round, ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 9 stitches**; join. | 54 each |

<div className="pumpkin-tip"><strong>🧶 A note for pattern testers</strong><p>The EPUB describes the FPdc placement as working around the “hdc directly below.” Some of its written repeat groups are terse relative to the totals printed for the round. I've preserved the source wording and totals instead of guessing a correction. Check the rib placement and stitch count on your actual bag as you go.</p></div>

<h2 id="shape-the-opening">Part 3 · Shape the rounded top</h2>

<figure className="instruction-photo pumpkin-feature-photo"><img src="/images/tutorials/pumpkin-halloween-bag/opening-shape.webp" alt="Five illustrated orange circles showing the opening narrowing from 54 to 30 stitches" width="960" height="680" loading="lazy"/><figcaption><span>SHAPE GUIDE</span> Follow the decrease rounds to taper the opening while keeping those familiar pumpkin ribs.</figcaption></figure>

From here, the decreases bring the sides gently inward. Keep using the same raised-stitch texture.

| Round | What to crochet | Total |
|---|---|---:|
| 15 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 4 stitches, hdc2tog, hdc in next 3 stitches**; join. | 48 |
| 16 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 8 stitches**; join. | 48 |
| 17 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 3 stitches, hdc2tog, hdc in next 3 stitches**; join. | 42 |
| 18 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 7 stitches**; join. | 42 |
| 19 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 3 stitches, hdc2tog, hdc in next 2 stitches**; join. | 36 |
| 20 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 6 stitches**; join. | 36 |
| 21 | Ch 2; repeat: **FPdc around the hdc directly below, hdc in next 2 stitches, hdc2tog, hdc in next 2 stitches**; join. | 30 |

<h2 id="finish-the-rim">Part 4 · Add a softly corded rim</h2>

<figure className="instruction-photo pumpkin-feature-photo"><img src="/images/tutorials/pumpkin-halloween-bag/rim-edge.webp" alt="Close-up illustration of a sturdy orange crocheted rim worked around the top of the bag" width="960" height="680" loading="lazy"/><figcaption><span>EDGE GUIDE</span> Reverse single crochet creates a visible corded finish around the top.</figcaption></figure>

**Round 22:** Ch 1, which **does not** count as a stitch. Reverse direction, working back across the stitches just made. Work **1 reverse sc in each stitch** around the opening, then join (**30 stitches**). Fasten off and weave in the yarn ends.

<h2 id="handle">Part 5 · Crochet the carrying handle</h2>

<figure className="instruction-photo pumpkin-feature-photo"><img src="/images/tutorials/pumpkin-halloween-bag/handle-strip.webp" alt="Illustration of a flat crocheted handle made of four rows of single crochet" width="960" height="680" loading="lazy"/><figcaption><span>HANDLE GUIDE</span> This simple strip is worked separately, in rows instead of rounds.</figcaption></figure>

Make the handle with the same orange yarn.

| Step | Instructions | Total |
|---|---|---:|
| Foundation | Chain 45. | — |
| Row 1 | Starting in the 2nd chain from the hook, sc in every chain across; turn. | 44 |
| Rows 2–4 | Ch 1 (does not count as a stitch), then sc across all 44 stitches; turn after each row. | 44 each |

Fasten off, leaving a long enough yarn tail to attach one end of the strap to the body.

<h2 id="assembly">Part 6 · Sew it together</h2>

<figure className="instruction-photo pumpkin-feature-photo"><img src="/images/tutorials/pumpkin-halloween-bag/attach-handle.webp" alt="Illustration showing a curved handle sewn to opposite sides of a pumpkin-shaped crochet bag" width="960" height="680" loading="lazy"/><figcaption><span>ASSEMBLY GUIDE</span> Attach each end of the strap securely on opposite sides of the rim.</figcaption></figure>

Lay the handle flat and check that it isn't twisted. Using the same orange yarn and a tapestry needle, sew its two ends to opposite sides of the pumpkin bag. Stitch back and forth through several rows to secure the points that bear the weight. Weave in all remaining tails.

<h2 id="helpful-notes">A few helpful notes</h2>

- **Want to personalize it?** Try a different shade of orange or stitch a small motif to the finished bag. These are optional ideas, not steps from the source pattern.
- **Using different yarn?** The finished dimensions can change with yarn, hook, and tension.
- **Carrying sweets?** The original pattern has no lining or closure. Test the handle seam and avoid overloading it.
