Every crochet project begins with a small moment: yarn on a hook, ready to become something new. Let's make your very first chain.

## Step 1 — Make a slip knot

1. Leave a short tail of yarn, about 15 cm (6 in).
2. Make a loop, then pull a bight of the working yarn through the loop.
3. Place the new loop on your hook and gently tighten it so the loop can slide freely.

## Step 2 — Hold your yarn and hook

Hold your hook in whichever way feels natural—like a pencil or a small knife. With your other hand, gently guide the working yarn to keep an even tension.

## Step 3 — Make a chain stitch

1. Bring the working yarn over your hook from back to front. This is a **yarn over**.
2. Pull that yarn-over loop through the loop already on your hook.
3. You just made **one chain stitch**! Repeat until you have a chain of about 15 stitches.

## Step 4 — Practice your rhythm

Look for little V shapes along your chain. Try keeping them approximately the same size—not too tight, not too loose.

> Beginner tip: Your first chain might curl, twist, or look uneven. That's normal. Make another one and notice how your hands grow more comfortable.

## What's next?

Once your chain feels comfortable, you're ready to learn the single crochet stitch and begin making rows.
