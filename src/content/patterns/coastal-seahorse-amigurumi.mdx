This small seahorse is full of lovely crochet details: a rounded head, a little projecting snout, a gently curling tail, a wavy spine and four bright fins. It's a rewarding project for anyone ready to try shaping and a mixture of crochet stitches.

The construction is adapted from **Sammy the Seahorse** by **A Morning Cup of Jo Creations (Josephine Wu)**, published in *Zoomigurumi 4* (2015). The instructions below walk through the process in Thread & Petal's step-by-step style.

## Read this before you start

The finished project is approximately **20 cm / 8 in tall when seated**, depending on yarn and tension. Use **US crochet terms**. The **head is made in an unjoined spiral**, whereas the **tail/body is crocheted in joined rounds** to keep the curve aligned. This distinction matters, so keep a marker nearby.

**Stitch key:** **MR** = magic ring, **sc** = single crochet, **inc** = 2 sc in one stitch, **dec** = single crochet decrease, **sl st** = slip stitch, **BLO** = back loop only, **FLO** = front loop only, **dc** = double crochet, **tr** = treble crochet, **hdc** = half double crochet, **ch** = chain.

New to circular crochet? Visit the [magic-ring lesson](/learn/magic-ring-crochet-in-the-round/) and [increase/decrease guide](/learn/crochet-increases-and-decreases/) first. For toys intended for very young children, embroider eyes instead of using detachable plastic features.

## Part 1 — Crochet the little snout and rounded head

Work with **light-blue yarn** in a spiral. The opening rounds make the small snout. The uneven increases then widen the face behind it, before the back of the head is drawn closed.

1. **Round 1:** Make 6 sc inside a magic ring. **[6]**
2. **Round 2:** Increase in every stitch. **[12]**
3. **Round 3:** Repeat an increase and 1 sc six times. **[18]**
4. **Round 4:** Work 18 sc in back loops only. **[18]**
5. **Round 5:** Crochet evenly around. **[18]**
6. **Round 6:** Repeat a decrease and 1 sc six times. **[12]**
7. **Round 7:** Work evenly around. **[12]**
8. **Round 8:** Increase in the next 6 stitches then work 6 sc in front loops only. **[18]**
9. **Round 9:** Repeat an increase and 1 sc six times then sc in final 6 stitches. **[24]**
10. **Round 10:** Repeat an increase and 2 sc six times then sc in final 6 stitches. **[30]**
11. **Round 11:** Repeat an increase and 3 sc six times then sc in final 6 stitches. **[36]**
12. **Round 12:** Repeat an increase and 4 sc six times then sc in final 6 stitches. **[42]**
13. **Round 13:** Work evenly around. **[42]**
14. **Round 14:** Repeat an increase and 6 sc six times. **[48]**
15. **Round 15:** Repeat an increase and 7 sc six times. **[54]**
16. **Round 16:** Repeat an increase and 8 sc six times. **[60]**
17–19. **Round 17–19:** Crochet evenly around in each round. **[60]**
20. **Round 20:** Repeat a decrease and 8 sc six times. **[54]**
21. **Round 21:** Repeat a decrease and 7 sc six times. **[48]**
22. **Round 22:** Repeat a decrease and 6 sc six times. **[42]**
23. **Round 23:** Repeat a decrease and 5 sc six times. **[36]**
24. **Round 24:** Repeat a decrease and 4 sc six times. **[30]**
25. **Round 25:** Repeat a decrease and 3 sc six times. **[24]**
26. **Round 26:** Repeat a decrease and 2 sc six times. **[18]**
27. **Round 27:** Repeat a decrease and 1 sc six times. **[12]**
28. **Round 28:** Make six decreases around. **[6]**

Before you close the head, place the eyes **between rounds 12 and 13**, on either side of the fuller face; use oval felt frames if desired. Stuff firmly as you decrease. Weave yarn through the final six loops and pull the opening closed.

## Part 2 — Form the curved tail and body

Use light-blue yarn again. **This part uses joined rounds**, rather than the spiral used for the head. At the end of every round, slip stitch into its first stitch; begin the next round with **ch 1** and work the first stitch into the stitch where you joined. **Do not count the joining slip stitch or opening chain as stitches.** This keeps the subtle shaping lined up along one side of the body.

Start at the narrow **tip of the tail** and work toward the neck. The early rounds are deliberately small, so don't stuff the tip.

## Rounds 1–17 — Build the thin tip

The increases happen on just one side, encouraging the tail to curve. Follow the joined-round method above after **every** round.

1. **Round 1:** Make 4 sc in a magic ring; join to the first stitch. **[4]**
2–3. **Round 2–3:** Ch 1 then sc once in each stitch; join. **[4]**
4. **Round 4:** Ch 1, increase once and work 3 sc; join. **[5]**
5. **Round 5:** Ch 1, increase once and work 4 sc; join. **[6]**
6. **Round 6:** Ch 1, work evenly around; join. **[6]**
7. **Round 7:** Ch 1, increase once and work 5 sc; join. **[7]**
8. **Round 8:** Ch 1, work evenly around; join. **[7]**
9. **Round 9:** Ch 1, increase once and work 6 sc; join. **[8]**
10. **Round 10:** Ch 1, work evenly around; join. **[8]**
11. **Round 11:** Ch 1, increase once and work 7 sc; join. **[9]**
12. **Round 12:** Ch 1, work evenly around; join. **[9]**
13. **Round 13:** Ch 1, increase once and work 8 sc; join. **[10]**
14. **Round 14:** Ch 1, work evenly around; join. **[10]**
15. **Round 15:** Ch 1, increase once and work 9 sc; join. **[11]**
16. **Round 16:** Ch 1, work evenly around; join. **[11]**
17. **Round 17:** Ch 1, increase once and work 10 sc; join. **[12]**

## Rounds 18–31 — Widen the belly and narrow the neck

Continue joining each round with a slip stitch, beginning each new round with **ch 1**. The shaped stitches all stay aligned to create the curved profile.

18. **Round 18:** Crochet 6 sc and then increase in each of the next 6 stitches; join. **[18]**
19. **Round 19:** Crochet 6 sc then repeat an increase and 1 sc six times; join. **[24]**
20. **Round 20:** Crochet 6 sc then repeat an increase and 2 sc six times; join. **[30]**
21. **Round 21:** Crochet 6 sc then repeat an increase and 3 sc six times; join. **[36]**
22. **Round 22:** Work one sc in every stitch; join. **[36]**
23. **Round 23:** Crochet 6 sc then repeat a decrease and 3 sc six times; join. **[30]**
24. **Round 24:** Crochet evenly around; join. **[30]**
25. **Round 25:** Crochet 6 sc then repeat a decrease and 2 sc six times; join. **[24]**
26. **Round 26:** Crochet evenly around; join. **[24]**
27. **Round 27:** Repeat a decrease and 2 sc six times; join. **[18]**
28. **Round 28:** Crochet evenly around; join. **[18]**
29. **Round 29:** Repeat a decrease and 1 sc six times; join. **[12]**
30. **Round 30:** Work 3 half double crochets then 9 slip stitches; join. **[12]**
31. **Round 31:** Crochet 12 sc evenly; join. **[12]**

Stuff the wider body and lower neck, leaving the curling tail tip unstuffed. Fasten off with a long sewing tail. Sew the neck to the head across approximately **head rounds 17–19**. Fold the thin tail into a curl and anchor it with a discreet sewing stitch.

## Part 3 — Crochet the blue wavy mane

The mane is a **single foundation chain** with eight little waves worked along one side. Use blue yarn and **chain 42**. The mix of short stitches and taller stitches makes the rippled edge. Work in order without turning the piece between waves.

1. **Wave 1 (toward the forehead):** Beginning in the second chain from the hook, work sc, dc, dc, sc, sl st. **[5]**
2. **Wave 2:** Work sc, dc, tr, tr, dc, sc, sl st. **[7]**
3. **Wave 3:** Repeat sc, dc, tr, tr, dc, sc, sl st. **[7]**
4. **Wave 4:** Work sc, dc, dc, sc, sl st. **[5]**
5. **Wave 5:** Work sc, dc, sc, then **mark the next stitch**; finish with 3 slip stitches. **[6]**
6. **Wave 6:** Work sc, dc, sc, sl st. **[4]**
7. **Wave 7:** Repeat sc, dc, sc, sl st. **[4]**
8. **Wave 8 (toward the tail):** Work 1 sc and 2 slip stitches. **[3]**

Keep a long yarn tail. Pin the **marked fifth wave** at the base of the head, where the neck joins. Stretch the beginning toward the forehead and the opposite end down the back. Center it carefully before sewing so that the mane divides the body evenly.

## Part 4 — Make two orange lower fins

Each lower fin is made directly into a **magic ring**, using different stitch heights to create one larger side.

1. **Round 1:** Into the ring work: **ch 2, dc, ch 2, sl st, ch 3, tr, ch 1, dc, ch 1, dc**.
2. Pull the ring snug and fasten off with a long sewing tail.
3. Make a second fin the same way. Place the larger half of each fin toward the top of the body and attach them around **body rounds 22–25**.

## Part 5 — Add two upper fins

These fins have their own slightly different stitch-height sequence. Again make **two** in orange.

1. **Round 1:** Into an adjustable magic ring work: **ch 3, tr, tr, ch 1, dc, dc, ch 2, sl st**.
2. Tighten the ring and fasten off, leaving a sewing tail.
3. Sew each upper fin to the side of the head, around **head rounds 24–27**.

## Part 6 — Add the seaside details

1. **Eye frames:** Cut two small oval shapes from white felt. Make a careful slit for each eye post before fastening the safety eyes, or use embroidered eyes instead.
2. **Mane decorations:** If you like the source's embellished look, sew a few small orange beads along the blue mane with coordinating embroidery thread. Omit beads for a child's toy.
3. **Belly patch:** Cut a soft egg-shaped felt patch, position it on the front of the rounded belly, and attach it securely with craft glue or hand stitches.
4. **Belly stripes:** Use orange embroidery thread to add short horizontal lines across the patch.
5. **Final shaping:** Add a last little stitch inside the curled tail if it starts to uncurl. Weave in all yarn ends.

**Helpful finishing note:** Because the upper body is joined to the head separately, pin the neck in place first and check the seahorse from both sides. The mane should line up with the face and the curve of the body.
