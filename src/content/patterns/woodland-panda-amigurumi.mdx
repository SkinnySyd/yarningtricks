Meet a gentle woodland friend with round black ears, little paw pads and a wonderfully cuddly silhouette. This panda amigurumi is a slow, satisfying make: a wide white head, black eye patches, a softly rounded body, and four paws that you build from separate pieces. The little grey paw pads are worth the extra stitches—they give this project its personality.

This Thread & Petal tutorial adapts **Bobo the Panda**, designed by **DIY Fluffies** for *Zoomigurumi 9*. All instructions below use **US crochet terminology** and walk through the construction piece by piece.

### Before you begin

- **Finished size:** approximately 21 cm / 8.5 in, with the recommended sport-weight yarn.
- **Skills:** magic ring, working in the round, increasing, decreasing, color changes and sewing small pieces.
- **Safety:** for gifts intended for children under three, choose embroidered eyes rather than plastic safety eyes.
- **Help anytime:** visit [magic rings and working in rounds](/learn/magic-ring-crochet-in-the-round/), [increases and decreases](/learn/crochet-increases-and-decreases/) or [finishing handmade pieces](/learn/fix-crochet-mistakes-and-finish/).

### Stitch key

**sc** = single crochet · **inc** = 2 sc in the next stitch · **dec** = single-crochet decrease · **MR** = magic ring · **sl st** = slip stitch. The number in square brackets is the stitch total at the end of the round.

## Part 1 — Create the rounded white muzzle

Start with the small, pale muzzle. Increase evenly until the circle is wide enough, then continue without shaping for four rounds to make its shallow sides. Leave a sewing tail and keep it ready for the face.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 3:** (make 1 single crochet, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 6 times **[18]**.
- **Rnd 4:** (make 2 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 6 times **[24]**.
- **Rnd 5:** (make 3 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 6 times **[30]**.
- **Rnd 6:** (make 4 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 6 times **[36]**.
- **Rnd 7:** (make 5 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 6 times **[42]**.
- **Rnd 8–11:** Work one single crochet into each of the 42 stitches **[42]**.

## Part 2 — Crochet both black eye patches

Make two matching dark circles. These sit under the safety eyes, adding the characteristic panda expression. Leave long ends for attaching them after you have positioned the snout.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 3:** Work two single crochets in each of the 12 stitches **[24]**.
- **Rnd 4:** Work one single crochet into each of the 24 stitches **[24]**.

## Part 3 — Add the little nose

Use black yarn to make a tiny two-round nose. It will be sewn to the center of the muzzle.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work one single crochet into each of the 6 stitches **[6]**.

## Part 4 — Shape the oversized panda head

## Part 5 — Make the soft, rounded ears

Crochet two identical black ears. They are left unstuffed, gently flattened and stitched to opposite sides of the head.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 3:** (make 1 single crochet, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 6 times **[18]**.
- **Rnd 4:** (make 2 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 6 times **[24]**.
- **Rnd 5:** (make 3 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 6 times **[30]**.
- **Rnd 6–10:** Work one single crochet into each of the 30 stitches **[30]**.

## Part 6 — Make the black-and-white body

Work the body from its white base upward, then introduce black yarn for the upper section. Keep your marker in place and stuff the body before fastening off.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 8 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[8]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 8 stitches **[16]**.
- **Rnd 3:** (make 1 single crochet, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 8 times **[24]**.
- **Rnd 4:** (make 2 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 8 times **[32]**.
- **Rnd 5:** (make 3 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 8 times **[40]**.
- **Rnd 6:** (make 4 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 8 times **[48]**.
- **Rnd 7–8:** Work one single crochet into each of the 48 stitches **[48]**.
- **Rnd 9:** (make 5 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 8 times **[56]**.
- **Rnd 10:** Work one single crochet into each of the 56 stitches **[56]**.
- **Rnd 11:** (make 6 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 8 times **[64]**.
- **Rnd 12–19:** Work one single crochet into each of the 64 stitches **[64]**.
- **Rnd 20:** (make 6 single crochets, decrease) repeat this sequence 8 times **[56]**.
- **Rnd 21:** Work one single crochet into each of the 56 stitches **[56]**.
- **Rnd 22–23:** Work one single crochet into each of the 56 stitches **[56]**.
- **Rnd 24:** (make 5 single crochets, decrease) repeat this sequence 8 times **[48]**.
- **Rnd 25–29:** Work one single crochet into each of the 48 stitches **[48]**.

After changing to black yarn, work rounds 22–29 in the black upper-body section and leave a long tail for joining the head.

## Part 7 — Crochet the two back legs

These rounded legs help your panda sit naturally. Make two identical pieces. **The source skips Round 23 after Round 22; this gap has not been invented or assumed here.** Follow the known rounds below and check the missing instruction against the designer before proceeding with the last decreases.

- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.

- **Rnd 22:** (make 5 single crochets, decrease) repeat this sequence 3 times **[18]**.

## Part 8 — Add six little toes to the back feet

For each hind foot, make three small crocheted toes. Stuff them and sew them near the rounded toe end, roughly across rounds 4–9.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 3:** Work one single crochet into each of the 12 stitches **[12]**.
### Finish the hind feet and pads

To finish the hind paws, make **six small grey paw spots**, each using 6 sc in a magic ring. Slip stitch to close, then attach one under each of the six toes. For the **two larger hind paw pads**, work 8 sc in a magic ring, increase in every stitch to reach **16 sc**, and attach one to the sole of each hind foot. Sew the finished hind legs to the sides of the body, approximately between body rounds 10 and 16.

## Part 9 — Make the front legs

The front legs are slightly slimmer than the hind legs. Work two pieces, add a little stuffing, and close the narrow tips.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 3:** (make 1 single crochet, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 6 times **[18]**.
- **Rnd 4:** (make 2 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 6 times **[24]**.
- **Rnd 5–9:** Work one single crochet into each of the 24 stitches **[24]**.
- **Rnd 10:** (make 2 single crochets, decrease) repeat this sequence 6 times **[18]**.
- **Rnd 11–14:** Work one single crochet into each of the 18 stitches **[18]**.
- **Rnd 15:** (make 4 single crochets, decrease) repeat this sequence 3 times **[15]**.
- **Rnd 16–20:** Work one single crochet into each of the 15 stitches **[15]**.
- **Rnd 21:** (make 3 single crochets, decrease) repeat this sequence 3 times **[12]**.
- **Rnd 22:** Work one single crochet into each of the 12 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 23:** Work 6 decreases **[6]**.

## Part 10 — Finish the front paws with toes

Make three smaller toes for each front paw. Attach them around the front edge of each leg.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 5 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[5]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 5 stitches **[10]**.
- **Rnd 3:** Work one single crochet into each of the 10 stitches **[10]**.
### Add the front paw-pad details

For the six front toe pads, place **5 sc** into a magic ring with grey yarn, slip stitch closed, and sew a pad beneath each toe. Make **two** larger palm pads (one for each front paw): place **6 sc** into a magic ring, then increase in every stitch for **12 sc**. Sew each pad to its paw. Attach the front arms near body rounds 24–28, keeping about eight stitches between the arms at the front.

## Part 11 — Add the fluffy white tail

Finish with the small white nub of a tail at the back.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** (make 1 single crochet, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 3 times **[9]**.
- **Rnd 3:** Work one single crochet into each of the 9 stitches **[9]**.
## Put your panda together

1. **Build the face before closing the head:** Sew the white muzzle across head rounds 14–28, gradually adding stuffing so it rises softly. Center the black nose on muzzle rounds 5–7.
2. **Set the eyes:** Pin the dark eye circles next to the muzzle across head rounds 13–23. Pass each safety-eye post through its patch and the head fabric; only then attach the washers. Stitch the patches down securely.
3. **Finish the head:** Continue the decreases from Round 31 onward, stuff firmly, and draw the last six loops closed.
4. **Attach the ears:** Flatten and position the two black ears along head rounds 8–20. Sew them down with neat small stitches.
5. **Join head and body:** Stuff the body, then stitch the opening of the body to about rounds 31–32 on the underside of the head. Adjust the head’s angle before tightening the seam.
6. **Position the paws:** Place the hind legs so the panda can sit comfortably. Add the long front legs at the upper body, and check that the grey pads face toward the viewer.
7. **Finish the tail:** Sew the small white tail on the back between body rounds 18–20. Weave in all loose yarn ends.

**Pattern check:** The original book omits **hind-leg Round 23** and proceeds from a 18-stitch Round 22 to a 12-stitch Round 24. Do not assume the missing round’s stitches. Confirm this with the designer or a test crochet before publishing the page as an error-free pattern.
