A tiny tropical bird can brighten an entire shelf. This crocheted toucan has a generous multicolor beak, a contrast face patch and sweet pale-blue feet. Its small body is full of playful details, so we’ll build it in stages and check the stitch totals as we go.

Our Thread & Petal adaptation follows the construction of **Toco the Toucan** by **Airali Design (Ilaria Caliri)** in *Zoomigurumi 9*. The descriptions are reorganized as a friendly, step-by-step tutorial using **US stitch terms**.

### Before you begin

- **Finished size:** about 14 cm / 5.5 in, using the source materials.
- **Level:** intermediate. The shaped back and joined toes deserve extra attention.
- **Working style:** continuous rounds unless explicitly noted. Use a stitch marker at the first stitch of each round.
- **Need a refresher?** [Magic rings](/learn/magic-ring-crochet-in-the-round/), [increases and decreases](/learn/crochet-increases-and-decreases/) and [reading patterns](/learn/how-to-read-crochet-patterns/) will help.

### Stitch key

**sc** = single crochet · **inc** = 2 sc into one stitch · **dec** = single-crochet decrease · **MR** = magic ring · **ch** = chain · **sl st** = slip stitch. Totals appear in square brackets.

## Part 1 — Make the oval white face patch

Begin with **16 chains**, working around both sides of the foundation rather than turning back in rows. The increases at the ends create a long oval. Finish the seventh round, then leave a sewing tail. Place the safety eyes between rounds 3 and 4 of the patch, but wait to secure the washers until the patch is aligned on the head.

- **Rnd 1:** Start in second ch from the hook, make 14 single crochets, 5 sc in last st. Continue on the other side of the foundation chain, make 13 single crochets, 4 sc in last st **[36]**.
- **Rnd 2:** (make 15 single crochets, make an increase in each of the next 3 stitches) repeat this sequence 2 times **[42]**.
- **Rnd 3:** Make 15 single crochets, (increase in next st, make 1 single crochet) repeat this sequence 3 times, make 15 single crochets, (increase in next st, make 1 single crochet) repeat this sequence 3 times **[48]**.
- **Rnd 4:** Make 15 single crochets, (make 2 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 3 times, make 15 single crochets, (make 2 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 3 times **[54]**.
- **Rnd 5:** Make 15 single crochets, (increase in next st, make 3 single crochets) repeat this sequence 3 times, make 15 single crochets, (increase in next st, make 3 single crochets) repeat this sequence 3 times **[60]**.
- **Rnd 6:** Make 15 single crochets, (make 4 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 3 times, make 15 single crochets, (make 4 single crochets, increase in next st) repeat this sequence 3 times **[66]**.
- **Rnd 7:** Work one single crochet into each of the 66 stitches **[66]**.

## Part 2 — Work the black body and shaped back

This is the largest part of the toucan. Begin with a magic ring, expand to 48 stitches, and on Round 15 make a **10-chain extension** that adds height to the back. Work up one side of that chain, around the body, and along the other side; this creates **66 stitches**. The next two rounds build the distinctive raised silhouette.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 3:** (increase in next st, make 1 single crochet) repeat this sequence 6 times **[18]**.
- **Rnd 4:** (increase in next st, make 2 single crochets) repeat this sequence 6 times **[24]**.
- **Rnd 5:** (increase in next st, make 3 single crochets) repeat this sequence 6 times **[30]**.
- **Rnd 6:** (increase in next st, make 4 single crochets) repeat this sequence 6 times **[36]**.
- **Rnd 7:** (increase in next st, make 5 single crochets) repeat this sequence 6 times **[42]**.
- **Rnd 8:** (increase in next st, make 6 single crochets) repeat this sequence 6 times **[48]**.
- **Rnd 9–14:** Work one single crochet into each of the 48 stitches **[48]**.
- **Rnd 15:** Chain 10, then starting with the second chain from the hook work 9 sc along that chain. Work 48 sc around the existing body, then return along the underside of the new chain with 9 more sc. **[66]**.
- **Rnd 16:** Work 3 sc in the first stitch, then 8 sc across one side of the raised back; work 48 sc around the body, followed by 8 sc on the other side of the raised back, then 3 sc in the final stitch. **[70]**.
- **Rnd 17:** Make 1 single crochet, increase in next st, make 66 single crochets, increase in next st, sc in last st **[72]**.
- **Rnd 18–32:** Work one single crochet into each of the 72 stitches **[72]**.

### Close the black body

After Round 32, make the final taper using these decreases. Stuff the bird gradually as the opening narrows.

- **Rnd 33:** Repeat *10 sc, then one decrease* six times. **[66]**
- **Rnd 34:** Repeat *9 sc, then one decrease* six times. **[60]**
- **Rnd 35:** Repeat *8 sc, then one decrease* six times. **[54]**
- **Rnd 36:** Repeat *7 sc, then one decrease* six times. **[48]**
- **Rnd 37:** Repeat *6 sc, then one decrease* six times. **[42]**
- **Rnd 38:** Repeat *5 sc, then one decrease* six times. **[36]**
- **Rnd 39:** Repeat *4 sc, then one decrease* six times. **[30]**
- **Rnd 40:** Repeat *3 sc, then one decrease* six times. **[24]**
- **Rnd 41:** Repeat *2 sc, then one decrease* six times. **[18]**
- **Rnd 42:** Repeat *1 sc, then one decrease* six times. **[12]**
- **Rnd 43:** Work six decreases to close the opening. **[6]**

Thread the yarn tail through the front loops of the remaining stitches and pull closed. Secure the yarn inside the toy.

Fold the oval face patch gently to fit the curve of the black body and position it across rounds 11–26 before the final decreases. Secure the eye washers only once the placement looks balanced.

## Part 3 — Shape the colorful beak

Work this curved beak from the red tip backward. Its carefully placed increases and decreases tilt one side without changing the total of 30 stitches. **Switch to pink before Round 10, then to yellow before Round 14**. Add stuffing before sewing the wide end to the face.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 3:** (increase in next st, make 1 single crochet) repeat this sequence 6 times **[18]**.
- **Rnd 4:** (increase in next st, make 2 single crochets) repeat this sequence 6 times **[24]**.
- **Rnd 5:** (increase in next st, make 3 single crochets) repeat this sequence 6 times **[30]**.
- **Rnd 6:** (decrease, make 1 single crochet) repeat this sequence 3 times, (increase in next st, make 6 single crochets) repeat this sequence 3 times **[30]**.
- **Rnd 7:** Work 3 decreases, (increase in next st, make 7 single crochets) repeat this sequence 3 times **[30]**.
- **Rnd 8–9:** Work one single crochet into each of the 30 stitches **[30]**.
- **Rnd 10–13:** Work one single crochet into each of the 30 stitches **[30]**.
- **Rnd 14–21:** Work one single crochet into each of the 30 stitches **[30]**.

Once the beak is finished, fasten off with a long sewing tail and stuff it lightly to hold its curved shape.

## Part 4 — Crochet the two cheeks

These little circles add the soft turquoise cheek accents on either side of the face.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 3:** (increase in next st, make 1 single crochet) repeat this sequence 6 times **[18]**.

Slip stitch once to smooth the edge of each cheek, and leave a long yarn tail for sewing.

## Part 5 — Make two compact wings

The wings are short flattened shapes. Do not stuff them; leave a long sewing tail on each.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 3:** (increase in next st, make 1 single crochet) repeat this sequence 6 times **[18]**.
- **Rnd 4–5:** Work one single crochet into each of the 18 stitches **[18]**.
- **Rnd 6:** (decrease, make 7 single crochets) repeat this sequence 2 times **[16]**.

## Part 6 — Make the center tail feather

The slightly longer central feather is the middle piece of the three-part tail. Work it narrow, then flatten it without stuffing.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 6 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[6]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 6 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 3–5:** Work one single crochet into each of the 12 stitches **[12]**.
- **Rnd 6:** (decrease, make 4 single crochets) repeat this sequence 2 times **[10]**.
- **Rnd 7:** (decrease, make 3 single crochets) repeat this sequence 2 times **[8]**.
- **Rnd 8:** (decrease, make 2 single crochets) repeat this sequence 2 times **[6]**.

## Part 7 — Make the two shorter tail feathers

Make two shorter black feathers. Arrange one on each side of the longer feather when sewing the tail.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 5 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[5]**.
- **Rnd 2:** Work two single crochets in each of the 5 stitches **[10]**.
- **Rnd 3–4:** Work one single crochet into each of the 10 stitches **[10]**.
- **Rnd 5:** (decrease, make 3 single crochets) repeat this sequence 2 times **[8]**.
- **Rnd 6:** (decrease, make 2 single crochets) repeat this sequence 2 times **[6]**.

## Part 8 — Crochet the narrow legs

Make two small turquoise tubes. Lightly stuff them and leave tails for attaching the feet.

- **Rnd 1:** Place 8 single crochet stitches into an adjustable magic ring **[8]**.
- **Rnd 2–8:** Work one single crochet into each of the 8 stitches **[8]**.
## Part 9 — Three-toed feet: the essential joining steps

Make **two feet**, with three toes for each foot.

**For the middle toe (make two total):**

1. **Round 1:** Place 6 single crochets into a magic ring. **[6]**
2. **Rounds 2–5:** Crochet one sc in each stitch in each round. **[6]**.

**For the side toes (make four total):**

1. **Round 1:** Make 6 sc into a magic ring. **[6]**
2. **Rounds 2–4:** Continue with 6 sc in each round. **[6]**

On each foot, finish one side toe and leave the second attached to the working yarn. You’ll join the three toes directly from that second toe.

**Join the toes, working in the source’s sequence:**

1. **Round 1:** Work 4 sc on the active side toe; leave the remaining stitch unused. On the middle toe, make 2 sc, skip one stitch, then work 5 sc on the other side toe. Skip one stitch on that toe, then one on the middle toe. Work another 2 sc on the middle toe, skip the next middle-toe stitch, skip one stitch of the side toe, and finish with 1 sc in the side toe. **[14]**
2. **Round 2:** Repeat *one decrease, then 5 sc* twice. **[12]**
3. **Round 3:** Make six decreases around the foot. **[6]**
4. **Round 4:** Work 6 sc evenly. **[6]**

Keep each foot flat and unstuffed. Cut the yarn and thread the tail through the front loops of the final six stitches; pull closed. Sew each foot to a narrow blue leg.

## Final assembly: give your toucan its personality

1. Fold the finished white face patch to check its curve. Position it across body rounds **11–26**, pass the safety eyes through both the patch and the body, and secure the washers. Stitch all around the white edge.
2. Sew the large stuffed beak starting at the top-center edge of the face patch. The yellow section should meet the face, with the pink and red sections extending outward.
3. Stitch the pale-blue cheeks on the lower sides of the white face area.
4. Pin and sew the wings to the sides around body rounds **22–28**, slanting them slightly toward the back.
5. Attach all three black feathers at the rear, with the longer feather in the center.
6. Position the legs underneath, approximately across body rounds **37–38**, and leave **five stitches between them**. Check that the finished feet make a stable sitting pose before sewing permanently.
7. Secure all joins with small stitches, then weave in the ends.

### A little help for the tricky parts

For the shaped back, count the new chain stitches separately from the body stitches. For the feet, check each skipped stitch before tightening the joins; skipping the wrong loop can change the final foot count. If you're uncertain, practice [counting stitches and keeping edges even](/learn/crochet-straight-edges/) first.
