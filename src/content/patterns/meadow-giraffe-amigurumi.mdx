A giraffe is a lovely amigurumi project because its character comes from simple shapes: a rounded head, a long neck, little horns and a pair of generous hooves. This seated version has a cheerful yellow coat with warm brown details. The pieces are worked separately and joined at the end, making it easy to see your progress.

The construction is adapted from **Stanley the Giraffe** by **Little Muggles**, published in *Zoomigurumi 4* (2015). Below, the instructions are organized into friendly, countable steps for Thread & Petal readers.

## Before you begin

The finished giraffe measures approximately **25 cm / 10 in** tall using the suggested yarn. All terms are **US crochet terms**. Work in continuous spirals unless otherwise indicated. Place a marker at the start of each round; numbers in square brackets show the final stitch count.

Need a refresher? Start with [single crochet](/learn/single-crochet-stitch/), [magic rings](/learn/magic-ring-crochet-in-the-round/) or [increases and decreases](/learn/crochet-increases-and-decreases/).

**Stitch key:** **sc** = single crochet; **inc** = 2 sc in one stitch; **dec** = crochet 2 stitches together; **BLO** = back loop only; **MR** = magic ring; **ch** = chain.

For toys intended for very young children, embroider the face rather than using small plastic eyes or buttons.

## Part 1 — Shape the yellow head

Begin at the crown and widen the circle in stages. The extra straight rounds create the plump face, while the final decrease rounds bring the neck opening inward. Use yellow yarn.

1. **Round 1:** Make 6 single crochets into an adjustable magic ring. **[6]**
2. **Round 2:** Increase in each of the six stitches. **[12]**
3. **Round 3:** Repeat 1 sc then an increase six times. **[18]**
4. **Round 4:** Repeat 2 sc then an increase six times. **[24]**
5. **Round 5:** Work one sc in every stitch. **[24]**
6. **Round 6:** Repeat 3 sc then an increase six times. **[30]**
7. **Round 7:** Repeat 4 sc then an increase six times. **[36]**
8. **Round 8:** Repeat 5 sc then an increase six times. **[42]**
9. **Round 9:** Work one sc in every stitch. **[42]**
10. **Round 10:** Repeat 6 sc then an increase six times. **[48]**
11. **Round 11:** Work one sc in every stitch. **[48]**
12. **Round 12:** Repeat 7 sc then an increase six times. **[54]**
13–17. **Round 13–17:** Work one sc into each stitch of every round. **[54]**
18. **Round 18:** Repeat 7 sc then one decrease six times. **[48]**
19. **Round 19:** Crochet evenly around. **[48]**
20. **Round 20:** Repeat 6 sc then a decrease six times. **[42]**
21. **Round 21:** Repeat 5 sc then a decrease six times. **[36]**
22. **Round 22:** Repeat 4 sc then a decrease six times. **[30]**
23. **Round 23:** Crochet evenly around. **[30]**
24. **Round 24:** Repeat 3 sc then a decrease six times. **[24]**
25–29. **Round 25–29:** Work one sc in each stitch in every round. **[24]**

Fit the eyes between rounds **13 and 14**, **11 stitches apart**. Stuff the head so it keeps its round shape, then cut the yarn with a long sewing tail. Do not close the neck opening.

## Part 2 — Add two little horns

Start each horn at the dark rounded tip, then switch to yellow to form the narrow stalk. Make **two**.

1. **Round 1:** With brown yarn, make 6 sc in a magic ring. **[6]**
2. **Round 2:** Increase into all six stitches. **[12]**
3. **Round 3:** Crochet once in every stitch. **[12]**
4. **Round 4:** Repeat 1 sc and a decrease four times. **[8]**
5–9. **Round 5–9:** Change to yellow and crochet 8 sc around in each round. **[8]**

Leave a tail for joining. Keep the horns unstuffed and attach them near rounds **4–5** of the head.

## Part 3 — Crochet the two pointed ears

Each ear begins as a tiny tube, becomes wider at the middle, then tapers again. Use yellow.

1. **Round 1:** Work 4 sc into a magic ring. **[4]**
2. **Round 2:** Single crochet around. **[4]**
3. **Round 3:** Increase in each stitch. **[8]**
4. **Round 4:** Crochet evenly around. **[8]**
5. **Round 5:** Repeat 1 sc then an increase four times. **[12]**
6. **Round 6:** Repeat 2 sc then an increase four times. **[16]**
7. **Round 7:** Repeat 3 sc then an increase four times. **[20]**
8. **Round 8:** Crochet once in every stitch. **[20]**
9. **Round 9:** Repeat 3 sc then a decrease four times. **[16]**
10. **Round 10:** Repeat 2 sc then a decrease four times. **[12]**
11. **Round 11:** Repeat 1 sc then a decrease four times. **[8]**

Flatten the ears without stuffing. Sew them to the sides of the head across rounds **9–11**, below the horns.

## Part 4 — Make the oval beige snout

Instead of beginning in a magic ring, this muzzle is worked **around both sides of a foundation chain** to form an oval. Start with **9 chains** and use beige yarn. As you go around each end, the increases round the corners. Keep the working direction consistent so the shape stays symmetrical.

1. **Round 1:** Starting in the second chain from the hook, work 7 sc, then an increase in the last chain. Turn along the underside of the foundation chain, make 7 sc, and increase in the final chain. **[18]**
2. **Round 2:** Repeat *(1 sc, inc, 6 sc, inc)* twice. **[22]**
3. **Round 3:** Repeat *(2 sc, inc, 7 sc, inc)* twice. **[26]**
4. **Round 4:** Crochet 26 sc evenly around. **[26]**
5. **Round 5:** Repeat *(3 sc, inc, 8 sc, inc)* twice. **[30]**
6. **Round 6:** Crochet 30 sc evenly around. **[30]**
7. **Round 7:** Repeat *(4 sc, inc, 9 sc, inc)* twice. **[34]**
8. **Round 8:** Crochet 34 sc evenly around. **[34]**
9. **Round 9:** Work 4 sc, dec, 8 sc, dec, 5 sc, dec, 9 sc, dec. **[30]**

Fasten off with a long tail. Stuff the snout lightly and sew it to the front of the head, spanning about rounds **14–23**. Add two embroidered nostrils or securely attached small buttons.

## Part 5 — Grow the yellow body and long neck

Begin at the bottom of the body. These rounds spread into a plump tummy, then taper into the long neck that supports the head. The stitching should remain firm enough to hold the stuffing.

1. **Round 1:** Make 6 sc in an adjustable magic ring. **[6]**
2. **Round 2:** Increase in all six stitches. **[12]**
3. **Round 3:** Repeat 1 sc then an increase six times. **[18]**
4. **Round 4:** Repeat 2 sc then an increase six times. **[24]**
5. **Round 5:** Repeat 3 sc then an increase six times. **[30]**
6. **Round 6:** Repeat 4 sc then an increase six times. **[36]**
7. **Round 7:** Repeat 5 sc then an increase six times. **[42]**
8. **Round 8:** Crochet evenly around. **[42]**
9. **Round 9:** Repeat 6 sc then an increase six times. **[48]**
10. **Round 10:** Repeat 7 sc then an increase six times. **[54]**
11. **Round 11:** Repeat 8 sc then an increase six times. **[60]**
12. **Round 12:** Repeat 9 sc then an increase six times. **[66]**
13–14. **Round 13–14:** Work 66 sc in each round. **[66]**
15. **Round 15:** Repeat 9 sc then a decrease six times. **[60]**
16–17. **Round 16–17:** Work 60 sc in each round. **[60]**
18. **Round 18:** Repeat 8 sc then a decrease six times. **[54]**
19. **Round 19:** Crochet evenly around. **[54]**
20. **Round 20:** Repeat 7 sc then a decrease six times. **[48]**
21. **Round 21:** Crochet evenly around. **[48]**
22. **Round 22:** Repeat 6 sc then a decrease six times. **[42]**
23. **Round 23:** Crochet evenly around. **[42]**
24. **Round 24:** Repeat 5 sc then a decrease six times. **[36]**
25. **Round 25:** Repeat 4 sc then a decrease six times. **[30]**
26–28. **Round 26–28:** Crochet evenly around. **[30]**
29. **Round 29:** Repeat 3 sc then a decrease six times. **[24]**
30–34. **Round 30–34:** Crochet evenly around. **[24]**

Begin stuffing while the opening is still wide; keep the neck supported without overfilling. Leave a generous sewing tail and join the head to the neck opening.

## Part 6 — Crochet the two arms

Each arm has a dark brown hand, a small contrasting edge, and a yellow upper arm. Work **two** pieces.

1. **Round 1:** With brown yarn, make 6 sc in a magic ring. **[6]**
2. **Round 2:** Increase around. **[12]**
3. **Round 3:** Repeat 1 sc then an increase six times. **[18]**
4. **Round 4:** Repeat 2 sc then an increase six times. **[24]**
5. **Round 5:** Work 24 sc in back loops only. **[24]**
6–7. **Round 6–7:** Crochet 24 sc in each round. **[24]**
8. **Round 8:** Repeat 2 sc then a decrease six times. **[18]**
9. **Round 9:** Change to yellow and work 18 sc in back loops only. **[18]**
10. **Round 10:** Crochet around. **[18]**
11. **Round 11:** Repeat the sequence 3 sc, decrease, 2 sc, decrease twice. **[14]**
12–14. **Round 12–14:** Crochet 14 sc around each round. **[14]**
15. **Round 15:** Repeat 2 sc and a decrease three times, then sc in last 2 stitches. **[11]**
16–17. **Round 16–17:** Crochet around. **[11]**
18. **Round 18:** Repeat 2 sc and a decrease twice, then sc in last 3 stitches. **[9]**
19–20. **Round 19–20:** Crochet 9 sc around. **[9]**

Stuff the hand and lower arm, leaving the uppermost section softer. Attach the arms about **nine body rounds below the head**.

## Part 7 — Make the two big sitting feet

The brown starting circle becomes a rounded hoof. Back-loop rounds form a neat transition into the narrower yellow leg. Make **two**.

1. **Round 1:** With brown yarn, work 6 sc into a magic ring. **[6]**
2. **Round 2:** Increase around. **[12]**
3. **Round 3:** Repeat 1 sc and an increase six times. **[18]**
4. **Round 4:** Repeat 2 sc and an increase six times. **[24]**
5. **Round 5:** Repeat 3 sc and an increase six times. **[30]**
6. **Round 6:** Repeat 4 sc and an increase six times. **[36]**
7. **Round 7:** Work 36 sc in back loops only. **[36]**
8–9. **Round 8–9:** Crochet around evenly. **[36]**
10. **Round 10:** Repeat 4 sc then a decrease six times. **[30]**
11. **Round 11:** Change to yellow and work 30 sc in back loops only. **[30]**
12. **Round 12:** Crochet evenly around. **[30]**
13. **Round 13:** Repeat 3 sc then a decrease six times. **[24]**
14–15. **Round 14–15:** Crochet evenly around. **[24]**
16. **Round 16:** Repeat 2 sc then a decrease six times. **[18]**
17–20. **Round 17–20:** Crochet evenly around. **[18]**
21. **Round 21:** Repeat 1 sc then a decrease six times. **[12]**
22. **Round 22:** Crochet evenly around. **[12]**

Stuff the feet and legs, keeping both the same firmness, and sew them near **round 8** of the body so the giraffe can sit.

## Part 8 — Crochet a small tail

The rounded brown tip is worked first, followed by a longer yellow stalk.

1. **Round 1:** With brown yarn, make 4 sc into a magic ring. **[4]**
2. **Round 2:** Crochet evenly around. **[4]**
3. **Round 3:** Increase in every stitch. **[8]**
4. **Round 4:** Crochet evenly around. **[8]**
5. **Round 5:** Repeat 1 sc and an increase four times. **[12]**
6. **Round 6:** Repeat 2 sc and an increase four times. **[16]**
7. **Round 7:** Repeat 1 sc and a decrease four times. **[12]**
8–15. **Round 8–15:** Change to yellow and work 12 sc around in each round. **[12]**

Lightly stuff the rounded end, then stitch the tail to the lower back near body round **8**.

## Part 9 — Make the three spots

The three dark brown circles give the back a cute, unmistakably giraffe-like finish. Make **three**.

1. **Round 1:** Make 6 sc in a magic ring. **[6]**
2. **Round 2:** Repeat *(1 sc, inc)* three times. **[9]**
3. **Round 3:** Repeat *(1 sc, inc, inc)* three times. **[15]**

Fasten off, leaving yarn to sew the spots to the back. Spread them out before securing; uneven spacing can look especially playful.

## Part 10 — Put your giraffe together

1. Sew the head firmly onto the narrow neck, centering the muzzle over the front of the tummy.
2. Add the two horns on the crown and the flattened ears slightly lower to either side.
3. Check that the eyes and nostrils sit evenly, then secure any remaining face details.
4. Pin the arms at the upper body and the legs at the lower sides, checking that the giraffe balances before sewing.
5. Sew on the tail and the three spots.
6. Tie on a ribbon or make a small fabric scarf if you'd like that finishing touch.

**A helpful tip:** If the neck leans, add a little extra stuffing around the neck opening before you finish sewing the head. For neat joins, review [our finishing guide](/learn/fix-crochet-mistakes-and-finish/).
