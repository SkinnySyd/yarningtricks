A slim bookmark is a lovely first project because every row gives you another chance to practice **single crochet**. This version has **25 stitches in each row** and **five rows in total**, plus an optional tassel. Its finished size depends on your yarn and tension.

This pattern uses **US crochet terminology**: *ch* = chain, *sc* = single crochet, and *st* = stitch. If those terms are unfamiliar, learn [the chain stitch](/patterns/first-crochet-chain/) and [single crochet](/learn/single-crochet-stitch/) before you begin.

## Before you start: two counting rules

- The loop on your hook is **not** counted as a stitch.
- The **chain 1 used to turn** at the end of each row **does not count as a stitch** in this pattern.

A stitch marker in the first or last single crochet can help you keep the edges straight. Our [straight-edges lesson](/learn/crochet-straight-edges/) explains exactly where those edge stitches sit.

## Crochet the bookmark

**Foundation chain:** Chain **26** loosely. You will work into the second chain from your hook, so your first row will have **25 single crochet stitches**.

**Row 1:** Insert your hook into the **second chain from the hook** and work 1 sc. Continue with 1 sc in each remaining chain. **Count: 25 sc.**

**Row 2:** Chain 1 and turn your work. Make 1 sc in the **first stitch** (do not count the turning chain), then 1 sc in each stitch to the end. **Count: 25 sc.**

**Rows 3–5:** Repeat Row 2 three more times. Each completed row should have **25 sc**. Your bookmark now has **five rows total**.

**Finish:** Cut the yarn, leaving a tail around **10–15 cm / 4–6 in**. Pull the tail through the last loop to fasten off, then weave the ends securely into the back of the bookmark.

## Optional: add a tassel

1. Cut three lengths of yarn, about **15 cm / 6 in** each.
2. Fold them together in half, making a loop at the fold.
3. Push the folded loop through a stitch at one short end of the bookmark.
4. Pull the loose ends through the folded loop and snug the knot gently.
5. Trim the tassel ends evenly.

Keep the tassel short if you want the bookmark to lie comfortably inside a book.

## Check your work

- **The edge is narrowing:** you may have missed the final stitch on one or more rows. Count 25 before turning.
- **The edge is widening:** you might be working into the turning chain as though it were a stitch. Work into the first real stitch instead.
- **The bookmark curls:** try a slightly larger hook or relax your tension. You can gently shape cotton after finishing, following the yarn label's care instructions.

For more help, visit [Crochet Straight Edges](/learn/crochet-straight-edges/) or [Fix Common Crochet Mistakes](/learn/fix-crochet-mistakes-and-finish/).

## Make it your own

Try a contrasting final row or a different yarn color. Once you can keep five rows even, you're ready to tackle bigger projects with the same stitches.
